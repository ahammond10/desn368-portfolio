What design choices did you make and why?

I went with dark green for the background and a lighter green for the sections because I wanted the page to feel like Pokémon Emerald. I made the headings yellow because I wanted them to look more like the Pokémon logo.

I liked how the pixel font looked next to the sprites, so I used it for the headings. For the directions, I went with Roboto because it was easier for me to read a whole paragraph in that font.

What was difficult to code? How did you solve it?

One thing I got stuck on was the tip in the shiny Mew section. It was all on one line, so I had to scroll sideways just to read the last few words.

I fixed it by splitting the tip into three lines in the HTML. The <pre> tag keeps the line breaks where I put them, so I could make the whole tip fit in the box.

How did you adapt your design for mobile?

My sidebar is 300px wide, which takes up most of a phone screen. At 640px or smaller, I put the links above the guide so there’s more room for the directions. The links wrap when they can’t all fit on one line.

I also hid the sprites so the checklists could use the whole width. At the bottom, I put the two round links one above the other so they’d fit on a phone.

What did you learn about Flexbox or CSS positioning?

I learned that display: flex goes on the box that holds both the sidebar and the guide. That puts them next to each other. On mobile, I could change the direction to column and the sidebar would go above the guide.

I also learned what align-self: flex-start does for my sidebar. It keeps the sidebar only as tall as its links. That gives it room to stick near the top while I scroll down to the next section.

What would you add or change with more time?

I’d add a map for Team Aqua’s Hideout. Right now I tell you to look for the room with four item balls, but if it’s your first time there, you still have to figure out which warp panels to take. I’d show that route on the map.

I’d also put a regular Mew next to shiny Mew in the last section. That way you could see the pink one and the blue one side by side and know which one to look for.
